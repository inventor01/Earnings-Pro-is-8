🥷 Delete Your Earnings Ninja Account

Applies to the Earnings Ninja app by Earnings Ninja (com.earningsninja.app)
SupportPrivacyTerms

You can permanently delete your Earnings Ninja account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it:

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Earnings Ninja app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Your account and data cannot be recovered.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you within 7 days.

📧 Request Account Deletion

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all data associated with it from our servers, including:

  • Your account and login credentials (email, password hash)
  • All earnings entries, tips, expenses, and mileage records
  • Goals, streaks, and progress history
  • App preferences, custom platforms, and custom entry types
  • Referral records and support/bug reports tied to your account

What is not deleted

  • Subscription billing records are held by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Minimal records we are legally required to retain (e.g., transaction records for tax or accounting compliance), which are kept only as long as the law requires.

Retention period

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests are completed within 7 days of identity verification. We do not keep backups of deleted account data beyond our standard rolling database backups, which expire automatically within 30 days.
